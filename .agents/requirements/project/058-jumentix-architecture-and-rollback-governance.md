# Requirement 058 - Jumentix Architecture and Rollback Governance

## Requirement

1. Current ownership boundaries are documented from the implemented `apps/*`
   and `packages/*` layout.
2. A change affecting ownership or deployment declares its rollback strategy in
   its Linear task and PR evidence.
3. Historical migration plans do not govern new work; their summary is
   `documentation/md/HISTORICAL-TRANSITIONS.md`.
